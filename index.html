Our Deep Multi-Step Email Growth Architecture

1. Abandoned Cart & Checkout Flows

When high-intent visitors abandon their carts, our intelligent 3-step psychological trigger sequence rescues lost sales automatically.

  • Email 1 (1 Hr): Friendly reminder addressing friction & shipping questions.
  • Email 2 (24 Hr): Strategic incentive with a limited-time discount code.
  • Email 3 (48 Hr): High-urgency scarcity notice before cart expiration.
